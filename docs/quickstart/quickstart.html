PlaneSpotter

The aircraft over your head, live.

Set up in 2 minutes

  1. Plug in the USB-C cable.Any phone charger works.
  2. Scan the code on the screenwith your phone's camera.
  3. Pick your Wi-Fi and type your town.Press Save. That's it.

The button

PressSwitch between Aircraft, ISS and Auto
Hold 3 sSettings: Wi-Fi, town, units, dark theme…
Hold 10 sErase everything and start again

The closest aircraft, its route and a radar of everything around you.

  • ● LIVE data is current
  • DELAYED a few seconds behind
  • OFFLINE screen: nothing shown is live; it says why
  • ★ rare aircraft and emergencies take over the screen
  • ISS 10 min before it's visible: where to look
Wi-Fi: 2.4 GHz only. Work networks with a username work; guest Wi-Fi with a sign-in page doesn't. Updates install by themselves. Data: adsb.lol, adsbdb.com, CelesTrak, GeoNames.
